2. Buying from us

2.1 The tees

Our tees are unisex heavyweight 100% cotton, garment-dyed. Garment-dyeing means the colour can vary slightly from tee to tee, and pictures on screen can differ a little from the real thing. Sizes and measurements are in the size chart on each tee's page.

2.2 Prices and payment

Prices are in pounds sterling and are the full price you pay for the tee. UK delivery is added at checkout and shown before you pay. Payment is taken by Stripe when you place your order. We never see or store your card details.

2.3 When your order is accepted

Your order is a contract with us once your payment goes through and you see the order confirmation. If we can't fulfil an order for any reason (for example, a size becomes unavailable), we'll tell you and refund you in full.

3. Delivery

  • We deliver to addresses in the United Kingdom only.
  • Tees are sent by tracked or standard post. Please allow 4–10 working days from ordering for yours to arrive.
  • If your order hasn't arrived after 15 working days, email us and we'll track it down or send a replacement.
  • The tee is yours, and our responsibility, until it reaches you.

4. Changing your mind (returns)

You have the right to cancel your order within 14 days, without giving a reason. Our tees come from our standard range and aren't personalised to you, so this right applies in full.

  1. Tell us within 14 days of the day your tee arrives. Email contact@waymark.it.com with your order details, or use the cancellation form below. You can also cancel before it arrives.
  2. Send the tee back within 14 days of telling us, to the return address we give you. Please send it unworn and unwashed with any labels still attached. You pay the return postage, and we'd suggest a tracked service.
  3. We refund you within 14 days of getting the tee back, or of you showing us proof of postage if that's sooner. The refund covers the tee and the original standard delivery charge, and goes back to the card you paid with.

You're welcome to try a tee on as you would in a shop. If it comes back worn, washed or damaged beyond that, we may reduce the refund to reflect the loss in value.

5. Faulty, damaged or wrong items

If your tee arrives faulty or damaged, or isn't what you ordered (wrong design, colour or size), email us within 30 days of it arriving, with a photo if you can. We'll send a replacement or give you a full refund, whichever you prefer, and we cover any postage. This doesn't affect your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, which also cover faults that show up later.

To keep the print looking its best, wash the tee inside out on a cool wash and don't iron over the print.
