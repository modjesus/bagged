Every hill you have climbed, marked on one map.

WayMark is a free hill-marking app for Britain. Tick the summits you have stood on, keep a journal of your days out, earn a cloth patch for the milestones, and follow the walkers you know.

The walkers you know

Follow friends, cheer an adventure, plan the next one from a recommendation.

There are two hundred and fourteen Wainwrights and two hundred and eighty-two Munros, and nobody climbs them in a weekend. A list is a slow thing. WayMark keeps it the way a notebook would, one summit at a time, so that the map fills in as the years do.
