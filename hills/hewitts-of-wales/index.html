The Hewitts of Wales

In the list137

Wales, over 2000 ft

The Hewitts of Wales are the 137 Welsh hills over 2,000 feet with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. They are concentrated in Eryri (Snowdonia) but reach south to the Brecon Beacons and east to the Radnor Forest. Hewitt stands for Hills in England, Wales and Ireland over Two Thousand feet.

  • Lowest2,001 ft
