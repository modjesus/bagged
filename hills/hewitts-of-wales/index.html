What are the Hewitts of Wales?

The Hewitts of Wales are the 136 Welsh hills over 2,000 feet with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. They are concentrated in Eryri (Snowdonia) but reach south to the Brecon Beacons and east to the Radnor Forest. Hewitt stands for Hills in England, Wales and Ireland over Two Thousand feet.

Make the list your own

Browse by area to find summits in the places you want to explore. Height is only one part of choosing a walk; terrain, route and conditions matter too.

In WayMark, mark the summits you have reached and keep your walking memories alongside your progress. Hills that belong to more than one list count towards each relevant list.

How many Hewitts of Wales are there?

This list contains 136 summits. Welsh hills of 2,000 ft or more with a drop of at least 30 m on all sides.

What is the highest summit on this list?

Snowdon is the highest, at 3,560 feet (1,085 metres), in Snowdon Massif.
