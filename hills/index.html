Which hill list should you choose?

Start with somewhere you enjoy walking. Explore the 214 Wainwrights in the Lake District, the 282 Munros in Scotland or the 95 Ethels in the Peak District. A shorter list means fewer summits, but it does not necessarily mean easier walking.

Lists use different rules. Some depend on height, some on prominence — the drop between a summit and the higher ground around it — and others are a named selection. The same summit can belong to several lists.

Can one hill count towards several lists?

Yes. Scafell Pike belongs to the Wainwrights, Birketts, Nuttalls, Marilyns, Hewitts of England and National Three Peaks. Mark it once in WayMark and it counts towards each.

Is WayMark free to use?

Yes. Explore and track the hill lists in WayMark for free. It works in your browser, with nothing to install.

How many hills are there in total?

These 21 lists cover 3,547 distinct hills, filling 5,790 places between them — a hill counts once on every list it belongs to, so the counts on the cards above add up to more than the number of hills.
