The Dales 30

Yorkshire Dales, over 2,000 ft

The Dales 30 are the 30 hills over 2,000 feet in and around the Yorkshire Dales, including the Howgill Fells. The list takes in the Three Peaks, the high Pennine whalebacks of Great Whernside and Buckden Pike, and the steep grass of the Howgills.
  • In the list30
  • Highest2,415 ft
  • Lowest2,001 ft
  • Areas2

Highest is Whernside. Lowest is Calf Top.
