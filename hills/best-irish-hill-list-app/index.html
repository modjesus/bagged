About the lists

The Arderins

Irish hills of 500 m or more with a drop of at least 30 m on all sides. There are 409. The highest is Carrauntoohil at 3,407 ft (1,039 m) and the lowest is Benbrack at 1,637 ft (499 m).

The five highest:

  1. Carrauntoohil, 3,407 ft (1,039 m)
  2. Beenkeragh, 3,308 ft (1,008 m)
  3. Caher, 3,281 ft (1,000 m)
  4. Knocknapeasta, 3,232 ft (985 m)
  5. Maolan Bui, 3,179 ft (969 m)

They also count towards: Vandeleur-Lynams (222 of the 409); Hewitts of the Republic (187 of the 409); Marilyns of Ireland (160 of the 409); Hewitts of Northern Ireland (20 of the 409). 131 of them are on no other list in WayMark.

Areas with their own page: Beara Peninsula (21), Blue Stack Mountains (16), Derryveagh, Glendowan (16), Dingle Peninsula - E of Dingle (17), Galty Mountains (15), Iveragh Peninsula S (32), Knockmealdown, Comeragh and Monavullagh Mountains (17), Leitrim and W Cavan (13), Macgillycuddy's Reeks (20), Maumturk Mountains (19), Mourne Mountains (26), Nephin Beg Range (14), Shehy Mountains (25), South Mayo and Central Galway (20), Sperrin Mountains (15), The Twelve Bens (14), Wicklow Hills N (27), Wicklow Hills S (21).

The Marilyns of Ireland

Irish hills with a drop of at least 150 m on all sides, regardless of absolute height. There are 452. The highest is Carrauntoohil at 3,407 ft (1,039 m) and the lowest is Puffin Island at 522 ft (159 m).

The five highest:

  1. Carrauntoohil, 3,407 ft (1,039 m)
  2. Knocknapeasta, 3,232 ft (985 m)
  3. Brandon Mountain, 3,122 ft (952 m)
  4. Lugnaquillia Mountain, 3,034 ft (925 m)

They also count towards: Arderins (160 of the 452); Vandeleur-Lynams (104 of the 452); Hewitts of the Republic (87 of the 452); Hewitts of Northern Ireland (12 of the 452). 292 of them are on no other list in WayMark.
