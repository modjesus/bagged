Marilyns of Ireland in Beara Peninsula

These are the 17 Marilyns of Ireland that lie in Beara Peninsula — 17 of the 452 on the full list. The highest is Hungry Hill at 2,247 feet (685 metres); the lowest is Disert at 673 feet (205 metres).

The Marilyns of Ireland are the 452 Irish hills with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides, whatever their height. Prominence alone decides, so the list runs from Carrauntoohil down to small coastal hills and islands that stand clear of everything around them.

How many Marilyns of Ireland are in Beara Peninsula?

There are 17, out of 452 on the full list.
