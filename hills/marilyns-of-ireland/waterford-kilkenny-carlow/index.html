Marilyns of Ireland in Waterford, Kilkenny, Carlow

These are the 15 Marilyns of Ireland that lie in Waterford, Kilkenny, Carlow — 15 of the 452 on the full list. The highest is Mount Leinster at 2,606 feet (794 metres); the lowest is Carrickbyrne Hill at 768 feet (234 metres).

The Marilyns of Ireland are the 452 Irish hills with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides, whatever their height. Prominence alone decides, so the list runs from Carrauntoohil down to small coastal hills and islands that stand clear of everything around them.
