Marilyns of Ireland in Dingle Peninsula - W of Dingle

These are the 14 Marilyns of Ireland that lie in Dingle Peninsula - W of Dingle — 14 of the 452 on the full list. The highest is Brandon Mountain at 3,122 feet (952 metres); the lowest is Inishtooskert at 561 feet (171 metres).

The Marilyns of Ireland are the 452 Irish hills with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides, whatever their height. Prominence alone decides, so the list runs from Carrauntoohil down to small coastal hills and islands that stand clear of everything around them.
