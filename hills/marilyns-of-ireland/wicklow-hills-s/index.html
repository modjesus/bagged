Marilyns of Ireland in Wicklow Hills S

These are the 21 Marilyns of Ireland that lie in Wicklow Hills S — 21 of the 452 on the full list. The highest is Lugnaquillia Mountain at 3,034 feet (925 metres); the lowest is Carrigroe Hill at 761 feet (232 metres).

The Marilyns of Ireland are the 452 Irish hills with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides, whatever their height. Prominence alone decides, so the list runs from Carrauntoohil down to small coastal hills and islands that stand clear of everything around them.

There are 21, out of 452 on the full list.
