Marilyns of Ireland in North East Donegal

These are the 16 Marilyns of Ireland that lie in North East Donegal — 16 of the 452 on the full list. The highest is Slieve Snaght at 2,016 feet (615 metres); the lowest is Croaghan Hill at 712 feet (217 metres).

The Marilyns of Ireland are the 452 Irish hills with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides, whatever their height. Prominence alone decides, so the list runs from Carrauntoohil down to small coastal hills and islands that stand clear of everything around them.

How many Marilyns of Ireland are in North East Donegal?

There are 16, out of 452 on the full list.
