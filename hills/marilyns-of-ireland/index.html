452Marilyns of Ireland.

The Marilyns of Ireland are the 452 Irish hills with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides, whatever their height. Prominence alone decides, so the list runs from Carrauntoohil down to small coastal hills and islands that stand clear of everything around them.

Common questions

How many Marilyns of Ireland are there?

There are 452. Irish hills with a drop of at least 150 m on all sides, regardless of absolute height.

What is the highest of the Marilyns of Ireland?

Carrauntoohil is the highest of the Marilyns of Ireland, at 3,407 ft (1,039 m), in Macgillycuddy's Reeks.

What is the lowest of the Marilyns of Ireland?

Puffin Island is the lowest of the Marilyns of Ireland, at 522 ft (159 m), in Iveragh Peninsula N.

How long does it take to complete the list?
