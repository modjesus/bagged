What are the Marilyns of Ireland?

The Marilyns of Ireland are the 452 Irish hills with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides, whatever their height. Prominence alone decides, so the list runs from Carrauntoohil down to small coastal hills and islands that stand clear of everything around them.

Make the list your own

In WayMark, mark the summits you have reached and keep your walking memories alongside your progress. Hills that belong to more than one list count towards each relevant list.

How many Marilyns of Ireland are there?

This list contains 452 summits. Irish hills with a drop of at least 150 m on all sides, regardless of absolute height.

What is the highest summit on this list?

Carrauntoohil is the highest, at 3,407 feet (1,039 metres), in Macgillycuddy's Reeks.
