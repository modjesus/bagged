Marilyns of Ireland in South Mayo and Central Galway

These are the 14 Marilyns of Ireland that lie in South Mayo and Central Galway — 14 of the 452 on the full list. The highest is Mweelrea at 2,672 feet (814 metres); the lowest is Maumfin at 564 feet (172 metres).

The Marilyns of Ireland are the 452 Irish hills with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides, whatever their height. Prominence alone decides, so the list runs from Carrauntoohil down to small coastal hills and islands that stand clear of everything around them.

How many Marilyns of Ireland are in South Mayo and Central Galway?

There are 14, out of 452 on the full list.
