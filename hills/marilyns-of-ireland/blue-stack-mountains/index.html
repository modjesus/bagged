Marilyns of Ireland in Blue Stack Mountains

These are the 25 Marilyns of Ireland that lie in Blue Stack Mountains — 25 of the 452 on the full list. The highest is Croaghgorm at 2,208 feet (673 metres); the lowest is Croaghegly at 804 feet (245 metres).

The Marilyns of Ireland are the 452 Irish hills with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides, whatever their height. Prominence alone decides, so the list runs from Carrauntoohil down to small coastal hills and islands that stand clear of everything around them.

How many Marilyns of Ireland are in Blue Stack Mountains?

There are 25, out of 452 on the full list.
