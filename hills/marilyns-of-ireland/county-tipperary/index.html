Marilyns of Ireland in County Tipperary

These are the 14 Marilyns of Ireland that lie in County Tipperary — 14 of the 452 on the full list. The highest is Keeper Hill at 2,269 feet (692 metres); the lowest is Ballincurra Hill at 1,322 feet (403 metres).

The Marilyns of Ireland are the 452 Irish hills with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides, whatever their height. Prominence alone decides, so the list runs from Carrauntoohil down to small coastal hills and islands that stand clear of everything around them.

How many Marilyns of Ireland are in County Tipperary?

There are 14, out of 452 on the full list.
