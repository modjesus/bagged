Marilyns of Ireland in Maumturk Mountains

These are the 18 Marilyns of Ireland that lie in Maumturk Mountains — 18 of the 452 on the full list. The highest is Barrslievenaroy at 2,303 feet (702 metres); the lowest is Derroura at 1,001 feet (305 metres).

The Marilyns of Ireland are the 452 Irish hills with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides, whatever their height. Prominence alone decides, so the list runs from Carrauntoohil down to small coastal hills and islands that stand clear of everything around them.

There are 18, out of 452 on the full list.
