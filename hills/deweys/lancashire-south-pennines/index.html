Deweys in Lancashire & South Pennines

These are the 26 Deweys that lie in Lancashire & South Pennines — 26 of the 425 on the full list. The highest is Black Hill at 1,909 feet (582 metres); the lowest is West Nab at 1,640 feet (500 metres).

The Deweys are the 425 hills of England, Wales and the Isle of Man between 500 metres and 609.6 metres, with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. They are named after Michael Dewey, and they sit directly below the Hewitts — the same drop rule, the next band of height down.

How many Deweys are in Lancashire & South Pennines?

There are 26, out of 425 on the full list.
