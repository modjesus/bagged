What are the Deweys?

The Deweys are the 425 hills of England, Wales and the Isle of Man between 500 metres and 609.6 metres, with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. They are named after Michael Dewey, and they sit directly below the Hewitts — the same drop rule, the next band of height down.

How many Deweys are there?

This list contains 425 summits. Hills in England, Wales and the Isle of Man at least 500 m high and below 609.6 m, with a drop of at least 30 m on all sides.

What is the highest summit on this list?

Craig Fach is the highest, at 1,997 feet (609 metres), in Snowdonia.
