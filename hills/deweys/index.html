425Deweys.

The Deweys are the 425 hills of England, Wales and the Isle of Man between 500 metres and 609.6 metres, with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. They are named after Michael Dewey, and they sit directly below the Hewitts — the same drop rule, the next band of height down.

Highest1,999ft
Lowest1,640ft
Areas22
PriceFree

Highest is Horse Head Moor. Lowest is Carraghan. Spread across England, Isle of Man, Wales.

Common questions

How many Deweys are there?

There are 425. Hills in England, Wales and the Isle of Man at least 500 m high and below 609.6 m, with a drop of at least 30 m on all sides.

What is the highest of the Deweys?

Horse Head Moor is the highest of the Deweys, at 1,999 ft (609 m), in the Central Pennines.

What is the lowest of the Deweys?

Carraghan is the lowest of the Deweys, at 1,640 ft (500 m), in Isle of Man.

How long does it take to complete the list?

It depends entirely on where you live and how often you get out. What matters more is keeping an accurate record, so you always know which of the 425 you have left. WayMark does that for free.

Is there a free Deweys tick list?

Yes. WayMark is a free web app that tracks all 425, plus 20 other hill lists across Britain and Ireland, on any phone or computer. Nothing to install and no charge.
