Deweys in South West England

These are the 13 Deweys that lie in South West England — 13 of the 425 on the full list. The highest is Hangingstone Hill at 1,984 feet (605 metres); the lowest is White Ridge at 1,659 feet (506 metres).

The Deweys are the 425 hills of England, Wales and the Isle of Man between 500 metres and 609.6 metres, with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. They are named after Michael Dewey, and they sit directly below the Hewitts — the same drop rule, the next band of height down.

How many Deweys are in South West England?

There are 13, out of 425 on the full list.
