Deweys in Radnor & Mid Wales

These are the 20 Deweys that lie in Radnor & Mid Wales — 20 of the 425 on the full list. The highest is Whimble at 1,965 feet (599 metres); the lowest is Moel Hywel at 1,657 feet (505 metres).

The Deweys are the 425 hills of England, Wales and the Isle of Man between 500 metres and 609.6 metres, with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. They are named after Michael Dewey, and they sit directly below the Hewitts — the same drop rule, the next band of height down.

How many Deweys are in Radnor & Mid Wales?

There are 20, out of 425 on the full list.
