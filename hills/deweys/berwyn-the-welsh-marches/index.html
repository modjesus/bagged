Deweys in Berwyn & the Welsh Marches

These are the 33 Deweys that lie in Berwyn & the Welsh Marches — 33 of the 425 on the full list. The highest is Glan-hafon at 1,994 feet (608 metres); the lowest is Moel Cae-howel at 1,667 feet (508 metres).

The Deweys are the 425 hills of England, Wales and the Isle of Man between 500 metres and 609.6 metres, with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. They are named after Michael Dewey, and they sit directly below the Hewitts — the same drop rule, the next band of height down.

How many Deweys are in Berwyn & the Welsh Marches?

There are 33, out of 425 on the full list.
