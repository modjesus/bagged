Deweys in Lake District

These are the 53 Deweys that lie in Lake District — 53 of the 425 on the full list. The highest is Illgill Head at 1,998 feet (609 metres); the lowest is High Dodd at 1,644 feet (501 metres).

The Deweys are the 425 hills of England, Wales and the Isle of Man between 500 metres and 609.6 metres, with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. They are named after Michael Dewey, and they sit directly below the Hewitts — the same drop rule, the next band of height down.

How many Deweys are in Lake District?

There are 53, out of 425 on the full list.

Do these count towards other lists?

Often, yes. A summit can belong to several lists at once, and marking it in WayMark counts it towards every list it belongs to.
