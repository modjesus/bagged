Hewitts of England in Yorkshire Dales

These are the 26 Hewitts of England that lie in Yorkshire Dales — 26 of the 181 on the full list. The highest is Whernside at 2,415 feet (736 metres); the lowest is Calf Top at 2,001 feet (610 metres).

The Hewitts of England are the 181 English hills over 2,000 feet with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. Hewitt stands for Hills in England, Wales and Ireland over Two Thousand feet. The English list runs from the Lake District and the Pennines to a single summit on Dartmoor.

How many Hewitts of England are in Yorkshire Dales?

There are 26, out of 181 on the full list.

Do these count towards other lists?

Often, yes. A summit can belong to several lists at once, and marking it in WayMark counts it towards every list it belongs to.
