What are the Hewitts of England?

The Hewitts of England are the 181 English hills over 2,000 feet with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. Hewitt stands for Hills in England, Wales and Ireland over Two Thousand feet. The English list runs from the Lake District and the Pennines to a single summit on Dartmoor.

Make the list your own

Browse by area to find summits in the places you want to explore. Height is only one part of choosing a walk; terrain, route and conditions matter too.

In WayMark, mark the summits you have reached and keep your walking memories alongside your progress. Hills that belong to more than one list count towards each relevant list.

How many Hewitts of England are there?

This list contains 181 summits. English hills of 2,000 ft or more with a drop of at least 30 m on all sides.

What is the highest summit on this list?

Scafell Pike is the highest, at 3,209 feet (978 metres), in Lake District.
