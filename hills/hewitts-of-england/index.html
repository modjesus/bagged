The Hewitts of England

England, over 2000 ft

The Hewitts of England are the 180 English hills over 2,000 feet with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. Hewitt stands for Hills in England, Wales and Ireland over Two Thousand feet. The English list runs from the Lake District and the Pennines to a single summit on Dartmoor.

  • Highest3,209 ft
  • Lowest2,001 ft
