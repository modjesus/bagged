Wainwrights in The Northern Fells

These are the 24 Wainwrights that lie in the Northern Fells — 24 of the 214 on the full list. The highest is Skiddaw at 3,054 feet (931 metres); the lowest is Latrigg at 1,204 feet (367 metres).

The Wainwrights are the 214 Lake District fells described by Alfred Wainwright in his seven Pictorial Guides to the Lakeland Fells, published between 1955 and 1966. They are not defined by height or prominence — they are simply the fells Wainwright chose to write up. Walking all 214 is one of Britain's best-known hillwalking challenges.

How many Wainwrights are in the Northern Fells?

There are 24, out of 214 on the full list.
