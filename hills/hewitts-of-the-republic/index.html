What are the Hewitts of the Republic?

The Hewitts of the Republic are the 187 hills in the Republic of Ireland over 2,000 feet with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. Hewitt stands for Hills in England, Wales and Ireland over Two Thousand feet; this is the Republic's share of that list, with the Northern Irish ones kept separately.

Make the list your own

How many Hewitts of the Republic are there?

This list contains 187 summits. Hills in the Republic of Ireland of 2,000 ft (609.6 m) or more with a drop of at least 30 m on all sides.

What is the highest summit on this list?

Carrauntoohil is the highest, at 3,407 feet (1,039 metres), in Macgillycuddy's Reeks.
