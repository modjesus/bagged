Hewitts of the Republic in Knockmealdown, Comeragh and Monavullagh Mountains

These are the 13 Hewitts of the Republic that lie in Knockmealdown, Comeragh and Monavullagh Mountains — 13 of the 187 on the full list. The highest is Kilclooney Mountain at 2,598 feet (792 metres); the lowest is Coumaraglin Mountain at 2,016 feet (615 metres).

The Hewitts of the Republic are the 187 hills in the Republic of Ireland over 2,000 feet with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. Hewitt stands for Hills in England, Wales and Ireland over Two Thousand feet; this is the Republic's share of that list, with the Northern Irish ones kept separately.
