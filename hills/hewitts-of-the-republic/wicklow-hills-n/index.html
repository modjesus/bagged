Hewitts of the Republic in Wicklow Hills N

These are the 15 Hewitts of the Republic that lie in Wicklow Hills N — 15 of the 187 on the full list. The highest is Mullaghcleevaun at 2,778 feet (847 metres); the lowest is Scarr at 2,100 feet (640 metres).

The Hewitts of the Republic are the 187 hills in the Republic of Ireland over 2,000 feet with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. Hewitt stands for Hills in England, Wales and Ireland over Two Thousand feet; this is the Republic's share of that list, with the Northern Irish ones kept separately.

How many Hewitts of the Republic are in Wicklow Hills N?

There are 15, out of 187 on the full list.
