Birketts in Southern Fells

These are the 78 Birketts that lie in Southern Fells — 78 of the 541 on the full list. The highest is Coniston Old Man at 2,635 feet (803 metres); the lowest is Water Crag at 1,001 feet (305 metres).

The Birketts are the 541 Lake District fells over 1,000 feet listed by Bill Birkett in Complete Lakeland Fells. The list covers every summit inside the national park boundary above that height, which makes it substantially longer than the Wainwrights and a natural next challenge after them.

How many Birketts are in Southern Fells?

There are 78, out of 541 on the full list.

Do these count towards other lists?

Often, yes. A summit can belong to several lists at once, and marking it in WayMark counts it towards every list it belongs to.
