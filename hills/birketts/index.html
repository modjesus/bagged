The Birketts

Lake District

The Birketts are the 541 Lake District fells over 1,000 feet listed by Bill Birkett in Complete Lakeland Fells. The list covers every summit inside the national park boundary above that height, which makes it substantially longer than the Wainwrights and a natural next challenge after them.

Common questions

How many Birketts are there?

There are 541. Lake District summits of 1,000 ft or more, as listed by Bill Birkett.

What is the highest?

Scafell Pike, at 3,209 ft (978 m), in Central & Western Fells.

What is the lowest?

Water Crag, at 1,001 ft (305 m).

How long does it take to complete the list?

It depends entirely on where you live and how often you get out. What matters more is keeping an accurate record, so you always know which of the 541 you have left. WayMark does that for free.

Is there a free Birketts checklist?

Yes. WayMark is a free web app that tracks all 541, plus 15 other British hill lists, on any phone or computer. Nothing to install and no charge.
