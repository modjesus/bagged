Vandeleur-Lynams in Iveragh Peninsula S

These are the 21 Vandeleur-Lynams that lie in Iveragh Peninsula S — 21 of the 275 on the full list. The highest is Stumpa Duloigh at 2,566 feet (782 metres); the lowest is Boughil at 2,070 feet (631 metres).

The Vandeleur-Lynams are the 275 Irish hills of 600 metres or more with at least 15 metres of drop on all sides. The drop rule is the most relaxed of the Irish lists, so it admits tops that the Arderins and the Hewitts both pass over.

How many Vandeleur-Lynams are in Iveragh Peninsula S?

There are 21, out of 275 on the full list.

Do these count towards other lists?
