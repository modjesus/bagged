Vandeleur-Lynams in Dingle Peninsula - W of Dingle

These are the 12 Vandeleur-Lynams that lie in Dingle Peninsula - W of Dingle — 12 of the 275 on the full list. The highest is Brandon Mountain at 3,122 feet (952 metres); the lowest is Beennabrack at 1,996 feet (608 metres).

The Vandeleur-Lynams are the 275 Irish hills of 600 metres or more with at least 15 metres of drop on all sides. The drop rule is the most relaxed of the Irish lists, so it admits tops that the Arderins and the Hewitts both pass over.

How many Vandeleur-Lynams are in Dingle Peninsula - W of Dingle?

There are 12, out of 275 on the full list.
