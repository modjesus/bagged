What are the Vandeleur-Lynams?

The Vandeleur-Lynams are the 275 Irish hills of 600 metres or more with at least 15 metres of drop on all sides. The drop rule is the most relaxed of the Irish lists, so it admits tops that the Arderins and the Hewitts both pass over.

How many Vandeleur-Lynams are there?

This list contains 275 summits. Irish hills of 600 m or more with a drop of at least 15 m on all sides.

What is the highest summit on this list?

Carrauntoohil is the highest, at 3,407 feet (1,039 metres), in Macgillycuddy's Reeks.

Can I get a free Vandeleur-Lynams checklist?
