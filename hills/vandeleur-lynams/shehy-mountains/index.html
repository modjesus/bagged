Vandeleur-Lynams in Shehy Mountains

These are the 15 Vandeleur-Lynams that lie in Shehy Mountains — 15 of the 275 on the full list. The highest is Mangerton Mountain at 2,750 feet (838 metres); the lowest is Gullaba Hill at 1,978 feet (603 metres).

The Vandeleur-Lynams are the 275 Irish hills of 600 metres or more with at least 15 metres of drop on all sides. The drop rule is the most relaxed of the Irish lists, so it admits tops that the Arderins and the Hewitts both pass over.

How many Vandeleur-Lynams are in Shehy Mountains?

There are 15, out of 275 on the full list.

Do these count towards other lists?

Often, yes. A summit can belong to several lists at once, and marking it in WayMark counts it towards every list it belongs to.
