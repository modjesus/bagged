Vandeleur-Lynams in Dingle Peninsula - E of Dingle

These are the 14 Vandeleur-Lynams that lie in Dingle Peninsula - E of Dingle — 14 of the 275 on the full list. The highest is Baurtregaum at 2,789 feet (850 metres); the lowest is Castle Hill at 1,969 feet (600 metres).

The Vandeleur-Lynams are the 275 Irish hills of 600 metres or more with at least 15 metres of drop on all sides. The drop rule is the most relaxed of the Irish lists, so it admits tops that the Arderins and the Hewitts both pass over.

There are 14, out of 275 on the full list.
