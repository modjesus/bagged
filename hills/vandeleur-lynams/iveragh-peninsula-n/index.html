Vandeleur-Lynams in Iveragh Peninsula N

These are the 13 Vandeleur-Lynams that lie in Iveragh Peninsula N — 13 of the 275 on the full list. The highest is Coomacarrea at 2,533 feet (772 metres); the lowest is Knocknadobar North Top at 1,975 feet (602 metres).

The Vandeleur-Lynams are the 275 Irish hills of 600 metres or more with at least 15 metres of drop on all sides. The drop rule is the most relaxed of the Irish lists, so it admits tops that the Arderins and the Hewitts both pass over.

How many Vandeleur-Lynams are in Iveragh Peninsula N?

There are 13, out of 275 on the full list.

Do these count towards other lists?
