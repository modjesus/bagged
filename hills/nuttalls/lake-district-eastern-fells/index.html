Nuttalls in Lake District, Eastern Fells

These are the 26 Nuttalls that lie in Lake District, Eastern Fells — 26 of the 442 on the full list. The highest is Helvellyn at 3,117 feet (950 metres); the lowest is Heron Pike at 2,008 feet (612 metres).

The Nuttalls are the 442 summits in England and Wales over 2,000 feet with at least 15 metres of drop on all sides. The list was published by John and Anne Nuttall. Because the drop rule is gentler than the Hewitts', it includes many subsidiary tops and is the longer of the two lists.
