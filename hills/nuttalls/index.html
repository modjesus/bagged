What are the Nuttalls?

The Nuttalls are the 442 summits in England and Wales over 2,000 feet with at least 15 metres of drop on all sides. The list was published by John and Anne Nuttall. Because the drop rule is gentler than the Hewitts', it includes many subsidiary tops and is the longer of the two lists.

Browse by area to find summits in the places you want to explore. Height is only one part of choosing a walk; terrain, route and conditions matter too.

How many Nuttalls are there?

This list contains 442 summits. Summits in England and Wales of 2,000 ft or more with a drop of at least 15 m on all sides.

What is the highest summit on this list?

Snowdon is the highest, at 3,560 feet (1,085 metres), in Snowdon Massif.
