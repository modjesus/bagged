England and Wales, over 2000 ft

Common questions

How many Nuttalls are there?

There are 442. Summits in England and Wales of 2,000 ft or more with a drop of at least 15 m on all sides.

What is the highest?

Snowdon, at 3,560 ft (1,085 m), in Snowdon Massif.

What is the lowest?

Mynydd Graig Goch, at 2,000 ft (610 m).

How long does it take to complete the list?

It depends entirely on where you live and how often you get out. What matters more is keeping an accurate record, so you always know which of the 442 you have left. WayMark does that for free.

Is there a free Nuttalls tick list?

Yes. WayMark is a free web app that tracks all 442, plus 15 other British hill lists, on any phone or computer. Nothing to install and no charge.
