Nuttalls in Carneddau

These are the 22 Nuttalls that lie in Carneddau — 22 of the 442 on the full list. The highest is Carnedd Llywelyn at 3,491 feet (1,064 metres); the lowest is Tal y Fan at 2,001 feet (610 metres).

The Nuttalls are the 442 summits in England and Wales over 2,000 feet with at least 15 metres of drop on all sides. The list was published by John and Anne Nuttall. Because the drop rule is gentler than the Hewitts', it includes many subsidiary tops and is the longer of the two lists.

How many Nuttalls are in Carneddau?

There are 22, out of 442 on the full list.

Do these count towards other lists?

Often, yes. A summit can belong to several lists at once, and marking it in WayMark counts it towards every list it belongs to.
