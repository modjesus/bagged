Donalds in Borders

These are the 44 Donalds that lie in Borders — 44 of the 89 on the full list. The highest is Broad Law at 2,756 feet (840 metres); the lowest is Herman Law at 2,014 feet (614 metres).

The Donalds are the 89 hills of the Scottish Lowlands over 2,000 feet, listed by Percy Donald in 1935. They cover the Southern Uplands — Galloway, the Borders, the Lowther Hills and the Pentlands — and are the least walked of the main Scottish lists, which is much of their appeal.

How many Donalds are in Borders?

There are 44, out of 89 on the full list.

Do these count towards other lists?

Often, yes. A summit can belong to several lists at once, and marking it in WayMark counts it towards every list it belongs to.
