The Donalds

In the list89

Scottish Lowlands

The Donalds are the 89 hills of the Scottish Lowlands over 2,000 feet, listed by Percy Donald in 1935. They cover the Southern Uplands — Galloway, the Borders, the Lowther Hills and the Pentlands — and are the least walked of the main Scottish lists, which is much of their appeal.

There are 89. Hills of the Scottish Lowlands at 2,000 ft or more, as listed by Percy Donald.
