What are the Donalds?

The Donalds are the 89 hills of the Scottish Lowlands over 2,000 feet, listed by Percy Donald in 1935. They cover the Southern Uplands — Galloway, the Borders, the Lowther Hills and the Pentlands — and are the least walked of the main Scottish lists, which is much of their appeal.

Make the list your own

Browse by area to find summits in the places you want to explore. Height is only one part of choosing a walk; terrain, route and conditions matter too.

In WayMark, mark the summits you have reached and keep your walking memories alongside your progress. Hills that belong to more than one list count towards each relevant list.

How many Donalds are there?

This list contains 89 summits. Hills of the Scottish Lowlands at 2,000 ft or more, as listed by Percy Donald.

What is the highest summit on this list?

Merrick is the highest, at 2,766 feet (843 metres), in Galloway.
