Donalds in Lowther Hills

These are the 12 Donalds that lie in Lowther Hills — 12 of the 89 on the full list. The highest is Green Lowther at 2,405 feet (733 metres); the lowest is Earncraig Hill at 2,005 feet (611 metres).

The Donalds are the 89 hills of the Scottish Lowlands over 2,000 feet, listed by Percy Donald in 1935. They cover the Southern Uplands — Galloway, the Borders, the Lowther Hills and the Pentlands — and are the least walked of the main Scottish lists, which is much of their appeal.

How many Donalds are in Lowther Hills?

There are 12, out of 89 on the full list.
