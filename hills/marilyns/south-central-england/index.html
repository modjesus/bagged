Marilyns in South Central England

These are the 12 Marilyns that lie in South Central England — 12 of the 1,550 on the full list. The highest is Dunkery Beacon at 1,706 feet (520 metres); the lowest is Nine Barrow Down at 656 feet (200 metres).

The Marilyns are the 1,550 hills in Britain with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides, whatever their height. The list was devised by Alan Dawson in 1992 and named as a play on Munro. Because it is defined by prominence alone, it includes both Ben Nevis and small coastal hills a few hundred feet high.

How many Marilyns are in South Central England?

There are 12, out of 1,550 on the full list.
