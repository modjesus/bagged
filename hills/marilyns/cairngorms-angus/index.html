Marilyns in Cairngorms & Angus

These are the 93 Marilyns that lie in Cairngorms & Angus — 93 of the 1,550 on the full list. The highest is Ben Macdui at 4,295 feet (1,309 metres); the lowest is Hill of Garvock at 909 feet (277 metres).

The Marilyns are the 1,550 hills in Britain with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides, whatever their height. The list was devised by Alan Dawson in 1992 and named as a play on Munro. Because it is defined by prominence alone, it includes both Ben Nevis and small coastal hills a few hundred feet high.
