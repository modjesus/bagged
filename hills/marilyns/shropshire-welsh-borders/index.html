Marilyns in Shropshire & Welsh Borders

These are the 23 Marilyns that lie in Shropshire & Welsh Borders — 23 of the 1,550 on the full list. The highest is Brown Clee Hill at 1,773 feet (540 metres); the lowest is Hegdon Hill at 837 feet (255 metres).

The Marilyns are the 1,550 hills in Britain with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides, whatever their height. The list was devised by Alan Dawson in 1992 and named as a play on Munro. Because it is defined by prominence alone, it includes both Ben Nevis and small coastal hills a few hundred feet high.

How many Marilyns are in Shropshire & Welsh Borders?

There are 23, out of 1,550 on the full list.
