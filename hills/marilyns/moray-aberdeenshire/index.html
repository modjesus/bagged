Marilyns in Moray & Aberdeenshire

These are the 36 Marilyns that lie in Moray & Aberdeenshire — 36 of the 1,550 on the full list. The highest is Morven at 2,861 feet (872 metres); the lowest is Waughton Hill at 768 feet (234 metres).

The Marilyns are the 1,550 hills in Britain with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides, whatever their height. The list was devised by Alan Dawson in 1992 and named as a play on Munro. Because it is defined by prominence alone, it includes both Ben Nevis and small coastal hills a few hundred feet high.

How many Marilyns are in Moray & Aberdeenshire?

There are 36, out of 1,550 on the full list.
