Marilyns in Central Belt

These are the 26 Marilyns that lie in Central Belt — 26 of the 1,550 on the full list. The highest is Ben Cleuch at 2,365 feet (721 metres); the lowest is Mount Hill at 729 feet (222 metres).

The Marilyns are the 1,550 hills in Britain with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides, whatever their height. The list was devised by Alan Dawson in 1992 and named as a play on Munro. Because it is defined by prominence alone, it includes both Ben Nevis and small coastal hills a few hundred feet high.

How many Marilyns are in Central Belt?

There are 26, out of 1,550 on the full list.

Do these count towards other lists?

Often, yes. A summit can belong to several lists at once, and marking it in WayMark counts it towards every list it belongs to.
