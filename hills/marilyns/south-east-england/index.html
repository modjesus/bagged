Marilyns in South East England

These are the 13 Marilyns that lie in South East England — 13 of the 1,550 on the full list. The highest is Walbury Hill at 974 feet (297 metres); the lowest is Detling Hill at 663 feet (202 metres).

The Marilyns are the 1,550 hills in Britain with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides, whatever their height. The list was devised by Alan Dawson in 1992 and named as a play on Munro. Because it is defined by prominence alone, it includes both Ben Nevis and small coastal hills a few hundred feet high.

How many Marilyns are in South East England?

There are 13, out of 1,550 on the full list.
