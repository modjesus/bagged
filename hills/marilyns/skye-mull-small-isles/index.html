Marilyns in Skye, Mull & Small Isles

These are the 86 Marilyns that lie in Skye, Mull & Small Isles — 86 of the 1,550 on the full list. The highest is Sgùrr Alasdair at 3,255 feet (992 metres); the lowest is Gometra at 509 feet (155 metres).

The Marilyns are the 1,550 hills in Britain with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides, whatever their height. The list was devised by Alan Dawson in 1992 and named as a play on Munro. Because it is defined by prominence alone, it includes both Ben Nevis and small coastal hills a few hundred feet high.

How many Marilyns are in Skye, Mull & Small Isles?

There are 86, out of 1,550 on the full list.
