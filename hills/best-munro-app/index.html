About the list

The Munros

Scottish mountains of 3,000 ft (914.4 m) or more, as listed by the Scottish Mountaineering Club. There are 282. The highest is Ben Nevis at 4,413 ft (1,345 m) and the lowest is Càrn Aosda at 3,002 ft (915 m).

The five highest:

  1. Ben Nevis, 4,413 ft (1,345 m)
  2. Ben Macdui, 4,295 ft (1,309 m)
  3. Braeriach, 4,252 ft (1,296 m)
  4. Cairn Toul, 4,236 ft (1,291 m)
  5. Sgòr an Lochain Uaine, 4,127 ft (1,258 m)

They also count towards: Marilyns (202 of the 282); National Three Peaks (1 of the 282). 80 of them are on no other list in WayMark.

Areas with their own page: Argyll (19), Cairngorms (54), Fort William (67), Kintail (24), Loch Lomond (14), Loch Ness (17), Perthshire (28), Skye (12), Torridon (17), Ullapool (24).

Questions

How many Munros are there?

There are 282 Munros. The highest is Ben Nevis at 4,413 ft (1,345 m) and the lowest is Càrn Aosda at 3,002 ft (915 m). Scottish mountains of 3,000 ft (914.4 m) or more, as listed by the Scottish Mountaineering Club.

Is there a free app for the Munros?

Yes. WayMark is free on any phone or computer, with no paid tier, and has every one of them. Walkhighlands, Munro Bagger, munros.app, Munro Companion and Munro Planner are free to start and say on their store pages that they cover the list.

Which Munro apps work on Android?

Walkhighlands and Munro Bagger are on Google Play and cover the list. WayMark works in the browser on Android, and you can add it to your home screen.

Which app has routes for the Munros?

Walkhighlands: yes, expert written routes across Scotland, offline with OS mapping. munros.app: yes, all 282, with Munros+. Munro Companion: yes, 350+ GPS routes with Companion+. Munro Planner: yes, a route catalogue, with GPX. Hill Lists: links to Walkhighlands and MountainViews. WayMark has 13 free routes reaching 13 of the 282 for the Munros.

Can one app track the Munros and other lists?

Yes. Hill Lists covers many lists at once. WayMark has 21, and one mark counts on every list a hill is on.
