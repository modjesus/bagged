95Peak District Ethels.

The Ethels are 95 hills and high points across the Peak District, chosen by the Peak District National Park to mark the centenary of Ethel Haythornthwaite, the campaigner whose work helped create Britain's first national park. They are not defined by height, and several can be walked in a single day.
