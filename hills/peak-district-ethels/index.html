What are the Ethels?

The Ethels are 95 hills and high points across the Peak District, chosen by the Peak District National Park to mark the centenary of Ethel Haythornthwaite, the campaigner whose work helped create Britain's first national park. They are not defined by height, and several can be walked in a single day.

Make the list your own

Browse by area to find summits in the places you want to explore. Height is only one part of choosing a walk; terrain, route and conditions matter too.

In WayMark, mark the summits you have reached and keep your walking memories alongside your progress. Hills that belong to more than one list count towards each relevant list.

How many Ethels are there?

This list contains 95 summits. Hills and high points across the Peak District, selected by the national park authority.

What is the highest summit on this list?

Kinder Scout is the highest, at 2,087 feet (636 metres), in Peak District.

Can I get a free Ethels checklist?

Yes. Use Print checklist on this page for a paper copy of all 95 summits, or open the list in WayMark to keep a digital record for free.
