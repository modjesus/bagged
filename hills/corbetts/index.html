The Corbetts

Scotland, 2500–3000 ft

The Corbetts are the 222 Scottish hills between 2,500 and 3,000 feet with at least 500 feet of drop on all sides. The list was compiled by John Rooke Corbett in the 1920s and 30s. Because the drop rule is strict, Corbetts are often steeper and more isolated than their height alone suggests.
  • In the list222
  • Highest2,999 ft
  • Lowest2,500 ft
  • Areas20

Highest is Beinn Dearg (Torridon). Lowest is Beinn na h-Uamha.

Common questions

How many Corbetts are there?

There are 222. Scottish hills of 2,500–3,000 ft with a drop of at least 500 ft (152.4 m) on all sides.

What is the highest?

Beinn Dearg (Torridon), at 2,999 ft (914 m), in Torridon.

What is the lowest?

Beinn na h-Uamha, at 2,500 ft (762 m).

How long does it take to complete the list?

It depends entirely on where you live and how often you get out. What matters more is keeping an accurate record, so you always know which of the 222 you have left. WayMark does that for free.
