What are the Corbetts?

The Corbetts are the 222 Scottish hills between 2,500 and 3,000 feet with at least 500 feet of drop on all sides. The list was compiled by John Rooke Corbett in the 1920s and 30s. Because the drop rule is strict, Corbetts are often steeper and more isolated than their height alone suggests.

Make the list your own

Browse by area to find summits in the places you want to explore. Height is only one part of choosing a walk; terrain, route and conditions matter too.

In WayMark, mark the summits you have reached and keep your walking memories alongside your progress. Hills that belong to more than one list count towards each relevant list.

How many Corbetts are there?

This list contains 222 summits. Scottish hills of 2,500–3,000 ft with a drop of at least 500 ft (152.4 m) on all sides.

What is the highest summit on this list?

Beinn Dearg (Torridon) is the highest, at 2,999 feet (914 metres), in Torridon.
