Corbetts in Loch Lomond

These are the 17 Corbetts that lie in Loch Lomond — 17 of the 222 on the full list. The highest is Beinn an Lochain at 2,956 feet (901 metres); the lowest is Meall an Fhudair at 2,507 feet (764 metres).

The Corbetts are the 222 Scottish hills between 2,500 and 3,000 feet with at least 500 feet of drop on all sides. The list was compiled by John Rooke Corbett in the 1920s and 30s. Because the drop rule is strict, Corbetts are often steeper and more isolated than their height alone suggests.

How many Corbetts are in Loch Lomond?

There are 17, out of 222 on the full list.

Do these count towards other lists?

Often, yes. A summit can belong to several lists at once, and marking it in WayMark counts it towards every list it belongs to.
