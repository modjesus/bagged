What are the Welsh 3000s?

The Welsh 3000s are the 15 summits in Eryri (Snowdonia) over 3,000 feet. They fall into three groups — the Snowdon massif, the Glyderau and the Carneddau — and traversing all of them in twenty-four hours is a long-standing mountain challenge.

Browse by area to find summits in the places you want to explore. Height is only one part of choosing a walk; terrain, route and conditions matter too.

How many Welsh 3000s are there?

This list contains 15 summits. Summits in Eryri (Snowdonia) of 3,000 ft or more.

What is the highest summit on this list?

Snowdon is the highest, at 3,560 feet (1,085 metres), in Snowdon Massif.
