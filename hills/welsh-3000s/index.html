15Welsh 3000s.

The Welsh 3000s are the 15 summits in Eryri (Snowdonia) over 3,000 feet. They fall into three groups — the Snowdon massif, the Glyderau and the Carneddau — and traversing all of them in twenty-four hours is a long-standing mountain challenge.

Common questions

How many Welsh 3000s are there?

There are 15. Summits in Eryri (Snowdonia) of 3,000 ft or more.

What is the highest of the Welsh 3000s?

Snowdon is the highest of the Welsh 3000s, at 3,560 ft (1,085 m), in the Snowdon Massif.

What is the lowest of the Welsh 3000s?

Tryfan is the lowest of the Welsh 3000s, at 3,002 ft (915 m), in Glyderau.

How long does it take to complete the list?
