About the list

The Marilyns

British hills with a drop of at least 150 m on all sides, regardless of absolute height. There are 1,550. The highest is Ben Nevis at 4,413 ft (1,345 m) and the lowest is Maol Domhnaich at 505 ft (154 m).

The five highest:

  1. Ben Nevis, 4,413 ft (1,345 m)
  2. Ben Macdui, 4,295 ft (1,309 m)
  3. Braeriach, 4,252 ft (1,296 m)
  4. Cairn Toul, 4,236 ft (1,291 m)
  5. Aonach Beag (Nevis Range), 4,049 ft (1,234 m)

They also count towards: Corbetts (222 of the 1,550); Grahams (219 of the 1,550); Munros (202 of the 1,550); Nuttalls (105 of the 1,550); Deweys (56 of the 1,550). 734 of them are on no other list in WayMark.

Questions

How many Marilyns are there?

There are 1,550 Marilyns. The highest is Ben Nevis at 4,413 ft (1,345 m) and the lowest is Maol Domhnaich at 505 ft (154 m). British hills with a drop of at least 150 m on all sides, regardless of absolute height.

Is there a free app for the Marilyns?

Yes. WayMark is free on any phone or computer, with no paid tier, and has every one of them. Marilyns (England), Y Marilyns, Marilyn Munros, Hill Bagging and BaggedIt are free to start and say on their store pages that they cover the list.

Which Marilyn apps work on Android?

Marilyns (England), Y Marilyns, Marilyn Munros, Hill Bagging and BaggedIt are on Google Play and cover the list. WayMark works in the browser on Android, and you can add it to your home screen.

Which app has routes for the Marilyns?

Marilyns (England): walk descriptions from the Relative Hills Society. Y Marilyns: walk descriptions from the Relative Hills Society. Marilyn Munros: walk descriptions from the Relative Hills Society. Hill Bagging: start points and waypoints shared by walkers. Hill Lists: links to Walkhighlands and MountainViews. BaggedIt: plan your own along mountain paths, GPX in and out. WayMark has 192 free routes reaching 87 of the 1,550 for the Marilyns.

Can one app track the Marilyns and other lists?

Yes. Hill Bagging, Hill Lists and BaggedIt cover many lists at once. WayMark has 21, and one mark counts on every list a hill is on.
