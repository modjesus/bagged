Grahams in Loch Lomond

These are the 19 Grahams that lie in Loch Lomond — 19 of the 219 on the full list. The highest is The Stob at 2,474 feet (754 metres); the lowest is Tullich Hill at 2,073 feet (632 metres).

The Grahams are the 219 Scottish hills between 2,000 and 2,500 feet with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides. The list is named after Fiona Torbet (née Graham), who published it in 1992. Many are quiet, little-visited hills with views out of proportion to their modest height.

How many Grahams are in Loch Lomond?

There are 19, out of 219 on the full list.

Do these count towards other lists?

Often, yes. A summit can belong to several lists at once, and marking it in WayMark counts it towards every list it belongs to.
