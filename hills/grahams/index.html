What are the Grahams?

The Grahams are the 219 Scottish hills between 2,000 and 2,500 feet with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides. The list is named after Fiona Torbet (née Graham), who published it in 1992. Many are quiet, little-visited hills with views out of proportion to their modest height.

Make the list your own

Browse by area to find summits in the places you want to explore. Height is only one part of choosing a walk; terrain, route and conditions matter too.

In WayMark, mark the summits you have reached and keep your walking memories alongside your progress. Hills that belong to more than one list count towards each relevant list.

How many Grahams are there?

This list contains 219 summits. Scottish hills of 2,000–2,500 ft with a drop of at least 150 m on all sides.

What is the highest summit on this list?

Beinn Talaidh is the highest, at 2,500 feet (762 metres), in Mull.

Yes. Use Print checklist on this page for a paper copy of all 219 summits, or open the list in WayMark to keep a digital record for free.
