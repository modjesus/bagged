The Grahams

Scotland, 2000–2500 ft

The Grahams are the 219 Scottish hills between 2,000 and 2,500 feet with at least 150 metres of drop on all sides. The list is named after Fiona Torbet (née Graham), who published it in 1992. Many are quiet, little-visited hills with views out of proportion to their modest height.
  • In the list219
  • Highest2,500 ft
  • Lowest2,005 ft
  • Areas21

Highest is Beinn Talaidh. Lowest is Cruach nam Mult.

Common questions

How many Grahams are there?

There are 219. Scottish hills of 2,000–2,500 ft with a drop of at least 150 m on all sides.

What is the highest?

Beinn Talaidh, at 2,500 ft (762 m), in Mull.

What is the lowest?

Cruach nam Mult, at 2,005 ft (611 m).
