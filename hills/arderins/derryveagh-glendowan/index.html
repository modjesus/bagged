Arderins in Derryveagh, Glendowan

These are the 16 Arderins that lie in Derryveagh, Glendowan — 16 of the 409 on the full list. The highest is Errigal at 2,451 feet (747 metres); the lowest is Crockfadda NE Top at 1,644 feet (501 metres).

The Arderins are the 409 Irish hills of 500 metres or more with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. The list takes its name from Arderin on the Laois and Offaly border, and it is the broadest of the Irish height lists — a 500-metre threshold reaches hills that the 600-metre lists leave out.
