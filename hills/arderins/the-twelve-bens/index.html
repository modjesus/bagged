Arderins in The Twelve Bens

These are the 14 Arderins that lie in the Twelve Bens — 14 of the 409 on the full list. The highest is Benbaun at 2,392 feet (729 metres); the lowest is Benglenisky at 1,703 feet (519 metres).

The Arderins are the 409 Irish hills of 500 metres or more with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. The list takes its name from Arderin on the Laois and Offaly border, and it is the broadest of the Irish height lists — a 500-metre threshold reaches hills that the 600-metre lists leave out.

How many Arderins are in the Twelve Bens?

There are 14, out of 409 on the full list.

Do these count towards other lists?

Often, yes. A summit can belong to several lists at once, and marking it in WayMark counts it towards every list it belongs to.
