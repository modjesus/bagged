409Arderins.

The Arderins are the 409 Irish hills of 500 metres or more with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. The list takes its name from Arderin on the Laois and Offaly border, and it is the broadest of the Irish height lists — a 500-metre threshold reaches hills that the 600-metre lists leave out.

Common questions

How many Arderins are there?

There are 409. Irish hills of 500 m or more with a drop of at least 30 m on all sides.

What is the highest of the Arderins?

Carrauntoohil is the highest of the Arderins, at 3,407 ft (1,039 m), in Macgillycuddy's Reeks.

What is the lowest of the Arderins?

Benbrack is the lowest of the Arderins, at 1,637 ft (499 m), in Leitrim and W Cavan.

How long does it take to complete the list?

It depends entirely on where you live and how often you get out. What matters more is keeping an accurate record, so you always know which of the 409 you have left. WayMark does that for free.

Is there a free Arderins tick list?

Yes. WayMark is a free web app that tracks all 409, plus 20 other hill lists across Britain and Ireland, on any phone or computer. Nothing to install and no charge.
