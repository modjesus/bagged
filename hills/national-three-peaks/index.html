The National Three Peaks

In the list3

Highest in England, Wales and Scotland

The National Three Peaks are Ben Nevis, Scafell Pike and Yr Wyddfa (Snowdon) — the highest mountains in Scotland, England and Wales. The challenge is to climb all three, usually within twenty-four hours including the driving between them.

Common questions

How many National Three Peaks are there?

There are 3. The highest mountain in each of Scotland, England and Wales.

What is the highest?

Ben Nevis, at 4,413 ft (1,345 m), in Scotland.

What is the lowest?

Scafell Pike, at 3,209 ft (978 m).

How long does it take to complete the list?

It depends entirely on where you live and how often you get out. What matters more is keeping an accurate record, so you always know which of the 3 you have left. WayMark does that for free.

Is there a free National Three Peaks tick list?

Yes. WayMark is a free web app that tracks all 3, plus 15 other British hill lists, on any phone or computer. Nothing to install and no charge.
