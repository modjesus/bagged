What are the National Three Peaks?

The National Three Peaks are Ben Nevis, Scafell Pike and Yr Wyddfa (Snowdon) — the highest mountains in Scotland, England and Wales. The challenge is to climb all three, usually within twenty-four hours including the driving between them.

What is the highest summit on this list?

Ben Nevis is the highest, at 4,413 feet (1,345 metres), in Scotland.
