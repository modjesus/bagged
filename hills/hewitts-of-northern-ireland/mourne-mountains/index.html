Hewitts of N. Ireland in Mourne Mountains

These are the 14 Hewitts of N. Ireland that lie in Mourne Mountains — 14 of the 20 on the full list. The highest is Slieve Donard at 2,789 feet (850 metres); the lowest is Slieve Loughshannagh at 2,024 feet (617 metres).

The Hewitts of Northern Ireland are the 20 hills over 2,000 feet with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. Almost all of them stand in the Mourne Mountains, with a handful in the Sperrins and one at Cuilcagh. It is the shortest of the Hewitt lists and the most achievable in a season.

There are 14, out of 20 on the full list.
