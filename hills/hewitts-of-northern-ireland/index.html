What are the Hewitts of N. Ireland?

The Hewitts of Northern Ireland are the 20 hills over 2,000 feet with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. Almost all of them stand in the Mourne Mountains, with a handful in the Sperrins and one at Cuilcagh. It is the shortest of the Hewitt lists and the most achievable in a season.

Make the list your own

How many Hewitts of N. Ireland are there?

This list contains 20 summits. Northern Irish hills of 2,000 ft or more with a drop of at least 30 m on all sides.

What is the highest summit on this list?

Slieve Donard is the highest, at 2,789 feet (850 metres), in Mourne Mountains.
