The Hewitts of Northern Ireland

Mournes and Sperrins

The Hewitts of Northern Ireland are the 20 hills over 2,000 feet with at least 30 metres of drop on all sides. Almost all of them stand in the Mourne Mountains, with a handful in the Sperrins and one at Cuilcagh. It is the shortest of the Hewitt lists and the most achievable in a season.

  • Highest2,789 ft
  • Lowest2,024 ft

Common questions

How many Hewitts of N. Ireland are there?

There are 20. Northern Irish hills of 2,000 ft or more with a drop of at least 30 m on all sides.

What is the highest?

Slieve Donard, at 2,789 ft (850 m), in Mourne Mountains.

What is the lowest?

Slieve Loughshannagh, at 2,024 ft (617 m).

How long does it take to complete the list?
