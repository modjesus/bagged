What are the Munros?

The Munros are the 282 Scottish mountains over 3,000 feet, first catalogued by Sir Hugh Munro in 1891. The list is maintained by the Scottish Mountaineering Club and has been revised several times as surveys have improved. Climbing all of them is known as Munro bagging, and completing the round is called compleation.

Make the list your own

Browse by area to find summits in the places you want to explore. Height is only one part of choosing a walk; terrain, route and conditions matter too.

In WayMark, mark the summits you have reached and keep your walking memories alongside your progress. Hills that belong to more than one list count towards each relevant list.

How many Munros are there?

This list contains 282 summits. Scottish mountains of 3,000 ft (914.4 m) or more, as listed by the Scottish Mountaineering Club.

What is the highest summit on this list?

Ben Nevis is the highest, at 4,413 feet (1,345 metres), in Fort William.
