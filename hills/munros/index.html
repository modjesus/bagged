The Munros

Scotland, over 3000 ft

The Munros are the 282 Scottish mountains over 3,000 feet, first catalogued by Sir Hugh Munro in 1891. The list is maintained by the Scottish Mountaineering Club and has been revised several times as surveys have improved. Climbing all of them is known as Munro bagging, and completing the round is called compleation.
  • In the list282
  • Highest4,413 ft
  • Lowest3,002 ft
  • Areas13

Highest is Ben Nevis. Lowest is Càrn Aosda.

Common questions

How many Munros are there?

There are 282. Scottish mountains of 3,000 ft (914.4 m) or more, as listed by the Scottish Mountaineering Club.

What is the highest?

Ben Nevis, at 4,413 ft (1,345 m), in Fort William.

What is the lowest?

Càrn Aosda, at 3,002 ft (915 m).

How long does it take to complete the list?
