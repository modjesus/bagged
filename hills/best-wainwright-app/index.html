About the list

The Wainwrights

The 214 Lake District fells Alfred Wainwright described in his seven Pictorial Guides. No height or prominence rule: inclusion was his judgement. There are 214. The highest is Scafell Pike at 3,209 ft (978 m) and the lowest is Castle Crag at 978 ft (298 m).

The five highest:

  1. Scafell Pike, 3,209 ft (978 m)
  2. Sca Fell, 3,163 ft (964 m)
  3. Helvellyn, 3,117 ft (950 m)
  4. Skiddaw, 3,054 ft (931 m)
  5. Great End, 2,986 ft (910 m)

They also count towards: Birketts (208 of the 214); Nuttalls (113 of the 214); Hewitts of England (96 of the 214); Marilyns (39 of the 214); Deweys (30 of the 214). 4 of them are on no other list in WayMark.

Areas with their own page: The Central Fells (27), The Eastern Fells (35), The Far Eastern Fells (36), The North Western Fells (29), The Northern Fells (24), The Southern Fells (30), The Western Fells (33).

Questions

Is there a free app to track the Wainwrights?

Yes. Wainwrights by Doug Colton is free on iPhone and Android. WayMark is free on any phone or computer, in the web browser, with no paid tier. Wainwright Companion and Fellbook are free to start, with paid extras.

Which Wainwright apps work on Android?

Wainwrights by Doug Colton is on Google Play. WayMark works in the browser on Android, and you can add it to your home screen. Wainwright Companion, Fells, Hill Lists and Fellbook are on Apple devices only.

Which apps track the 116 Outlying Fells?

Wainwrights by Doug Colton, Hill Lists, Fellbook, and Wainwright Companion with Companion+. WayMark doesn’t have them yet.

Which app has routes for the Wainwrights?

Wainwright Companion has 275+ routes with Companion+ (£8.95 once). WayMark has 240 free routes covering all 214 Wainwrights, each with a GPX file. Hill Lists links to routes on Walkhighlands.

Can one app track the Wainwrights and the Munros?

Yes. Hill Lists has over 90 lists. WayMark has 21, including the Wainwrights, Birketts, Nuttalls, Munros and Marilyns, and one mark counts on every list a hill is on.
