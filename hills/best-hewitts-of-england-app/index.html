About the list

The Hewitts of England

English hills of 2,000 ft or more with a drop of at least 30 m on all sides. There are 181. The highest is Scafell Pike at 3,209 ft (978 m) and the lowest is Calf Top at 2,001 ft (610 m).

The five highest:

  1. Scafell Pike, 3,209 ft (978 m)
  2. Sca Fell, 3,163 ft (964 m)
  3. Helvellyn, 3,117 ft (950 m)
  4. Ill Crag, 3,068 ft (935 m)
  5. Broad Crag, 3,064 ft (934 m)

They also count towards: Nuttalls (181 of the 181); Birketts (112 of the 181); Wainwrights (96 of the 181); Marilyns (52 of the 181); Dales 30 (30 of the 181).

Areas with their own page: Lake District (114), North Pennines (26), Yorkshire Dales (26).
