What are the Yorkshire Three Peaks?

The Yorkshire Three Peaks are Pen-y-ghent, Whernside and Ingleborough, the three hills that ring the head of Ribblesdale. Walking all three in a single circuit is one of Britain's most popular hill challenges, traditionally done inside twelve hours.

How many Yorkshire Three Peaks are there?

This list contains 3 summits. Pen-y-ghent, Whernside and Ingleborough, walked as one circuit from Horton in Ribblesdale.

What is the highest summit on this list?

Whernside is the highest, at 2,415 feet (736 metres), in Yorkshire Dales.
