The Yorkshire Three Peaks

Ingleborough, Whernside and Pen-y-ghent

The Yorkshire Three Peaks are Pen-y-ghent, Whernside and Ingleborough, the three hills that ring the head of Ribblesdale. Walking all three in a single circuit is one of Britain's most popular hill challenges, traditionally done inside twelve hours.

Common questions

How many Yorkshire Three Peaks are there?

There are 3. Pen-y-ghent, Whernside and Ingleborough, walked as one circuit from Horton in Ribblesdale.

What is the highest?

Whernside, at 2,415 ft (736 m), in Yorkshire Dales.

What is the lowest?

Pen-y-ghent, at 2,277 ft (694 m).

How long does it take to complete the list?

It depends entirely on where you live and how often you get out. What matters more is keeping an accurate record, so you always know which of the 3 you have left. WayMark does that for free.

Is there a free Yorkshire Three Peaks checklist?

Yes. WayMark is a free web app that tracks all 3, plus 15 other British hill lists, on any phone or computer. Nothing to install and no charge.
